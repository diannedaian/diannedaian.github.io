Rules and model

Both sides start with 5 lives, 10 gold and separate shops. A loss costs 1 life; a draw costs none. Round 3 restores up to 1 life. The match ends when either side has no lives or reaches 10 wins.

Each shop allows 30 actions before battle is forced. Matches end after at most 40 rounds; reaching that limit is not a win.

Model A (85101): 341,516 frozen parameters. The browser uses the same rules and legal-action mask, choosing the highest-probability move. No GPU or further training is needed.

This is a frozen v0.46-inspired sandbox, not the official client. Direct duels differ from the benchmark: 82.27% was the three-model mean ten-win episode success against opponent pools, not a human win rate.

The match stays in this page and is lost on refresh. The current AI shop is hidden; its previous-round decisions are revealed after battle.
