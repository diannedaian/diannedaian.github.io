Model Anything in Real Time

DreamGrid

What it does

DreamGrid turns room measurements and a budget into a game-like, correctly scaled 3D space that users can decorate and shop from. Users can place, rotate, snap, stack, and inspect furniture; add windows and doors; measure clearances; and preview how the room changes throughout the day.

A furniture photo is analyzed into a constrained specification, then converted by a trusted Blender pipeline into a real-scale GLB model. DreamGrid can also find real products through Google Shopping, track the room's live subtotal, recommend cheaper alternatives, and approve the final shopping plan with a passkey before completing a Visa sandbox transaction.
