Project Overview

Tea Time in Five is a charming 3D platformer where you play as a tiny piece of cookie dough climbing a tower of sweets. Collect decorations to become prettier, but be careful—prettiness comes with a cost!

Built for the Brackeys Game Jam 2025.2, this project showcases 3D art creation, Unity gameplay programming, and collaborative game development.
